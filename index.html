01 / Why Future Skills?

The future is not fixed.
Learn to shape it.

Future Skills Simulator brings strategic foresight into vocational education and training (VET). It helps learners, educators and entrepreneurs move from uncertainty to informed action.

01 / ANTICIPATE

Foresight literacy

Recognise emerging signals, explore alternative futures and turn uncertainty into practical career planning.

02 / ADAPT

Digital transformation

Understand automation, emerging technologies and Industry 4.0/5.0 transitions through accessible simulations.

03 / TRANSFORM

Green & strategic skills

Explore sustainable business models, climate-related transitions and innovation-driven professional pathways.

Designed for VET learners (primarily aged 18–35), trainers, SME mentors and local stakeholders, with attention to learners with fewer opportunities.

02 / Interactive learning

04 / Project journey

From research
to real-world learning.

October 2026 – March 2028. Two complementary activities connect methodological development with practical application in Italy and Lithuania.

Oct 2026 – Jun 2027

01. Develop the Simulator Pack

Needs assessment, curriculum design, simulator logic, educators' toolkit and consultative validation with VET trainers. Led by INNES.

Scheduled activity
Nov 2027 – Mar 2028

02. Local Learning Labs

12 planned workshops, two multiplier events and a final transnational online exchange. Participants will develop future skills roadmaps. Led by Ekosofija.

Scheduled activity
Project targets, not achieved results: 60 VET learners, 20 trainers and SME mentors, and 50 stakeholders.
